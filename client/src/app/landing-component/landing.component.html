How It Works

A complete claim lifecycle in 4 roles

Every stage is mapped to a role to ensure accountability and faster resolution.

01

Policyholder

Files a claim and tracks the live status updates from the dashboard.

02

Adjuster

Validates claims and assigns them for investigation or underwriter review.

03

Investigator

Creates investigation reports and updates investigation status for the claim.

04

Underwriter

Reviews evidence and finalizes the decision: Approved or Rejected.

Role-Based Portal

Designed for every user of ClaimSurePro

Each role gets a focused dashboard with only relevant actions.

Policyholder

  • Submit claims
  • View claim history
  • Track status

Investigator

Underwriter

  • Finalize decision
